## 3. Portfolio item

**Title:** Kanzagh Accounting: a complete accounting system in Microsoft Access

**Your role:** Designer and sole developer

**Description:**
Kanzagh Accounting is a full accounting system for Canadian small businesses, built in Microsoft Access and VBA.

It covers the general ledger, receivables, payables, inventory with average or FIFO costing, multi-currency, payroll with CPP/QPP, EI and income tax, and year-end closing. Every invoice, payment and paycheque is posted as a balanced journal entry that can be traced back to its document and reversed.

For government filing it writes T4, T4A and T5018 XML, checked against the CRA's published schemas, a Record of Employment file checked against Service Canada's schema, and a CPA-005 direct-deposit file for the bank.

It is built to be handed to a client: a set-up wizard, a licence key for each customer, modules that can be switched on or off, user rights by area, English and French screens, a multi-user edition, and data files that upgrade themselves when a new version is installed.

## 7. What not to claim

- Do not say the program is certified or approved by the CRA, Service Canada or Revenu Quebec. Say: "writes files in the published formats".
- Do not name other accounting products or compare with them.
- Do not promise Quebec RL-1 electronic filing: it is not built.
- Do not say payroll results are guaranteed. Say the client's accountant should review the first pay run.
- The company in the pictures is sample data. Say so if a client asks.
